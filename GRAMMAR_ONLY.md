# Grammar-only AI check

After handwriting and transcribing a note, paste the transcription into an AI
tool with this prompt:

> Correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and obvious word-choice errors in
> the text below. Preserve my voice, structure, technical meaning, uncertainty,
> and level of detail. Do not add ideas, facts, examples, explanations, or a
> summary. Return (1) the minimally corrected text and (2) a short bullet list
> of the corrections. If a sentence is unclear, flag it without rewriting the
> idea.
>
> Text to check:

Do not ask the AI to summarize or improve the content. The handwritten draft is
the learning; the AI is only a copy editor. Commit the minimally corrected
transcription to `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
